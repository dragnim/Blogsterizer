What do these mean?
Safe
Deterministic clean-up the app already applied to your source: removing code-line, adding ex-link, migrating a configured URL. Nothing for you to do. This number reflects your original source and does not change as you work.
Suggestion
Something a human may want to change, such as splitting a long paragraph. Never applied automatically. Where the app can make the change for you, there is a button.
Warning
Something suspicious but not necessarily wrong, such as a second <h1>. Needs your decision.
Error
A core rule failed. Copy and download are disabled until it is resolved.
Fixed
Changes you chose to apply. Undo puts the document back.
